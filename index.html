      Every durian PBZ trial

      PBZ is the main off-season tool, but results vary. The positive results came from foliar sprays at 1,000–1,600 ppm on fully expanded leaves, followed by dry weather.

      How much PBZ goes in my tank?

      1. Your tank
      2. Your PBZ product (strength on the label, % or g/L)

      Check the strength printed on your bottle. Choosing 10% when the bottle says 25% puts 2.5 times too much PBZ in the tank.

      3. Which trees
      4. Your orchard

      Spray until the leaves are wet but not dripping. An 8–10-year tree needs about 10–12 L (Hâu & Hiếu 2026); the trials used about 20 L on big trees.

      Scientist view (ppm)

      "Mature trees" = 1,000 ppm, "Young trees" = 750 ppm, "Hard to flower" = 1,500 ppm. 1,000 ppm means 1 g of active ingredient in every litre of water.

      Safety, residue and sustainability

        Triazole fungicides: do they act like PBZ?

        PBZ is a triazole, like several common fungicides. Some of them slow growth a little; none has been shown to induce durian flowering.
