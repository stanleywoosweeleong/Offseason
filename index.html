      Every durian PBZ trial

      PBZ is the main off-season tool, but results vary. The positive results came from foliar sprays at 1,000–1,600 ppm on fully expanded leaves, followed by dry weather.

      Spray-tank calculator

      Book minimum for an 8–10-year tree: 10–12 L, about 12–15 g a.i. Trials used about 20 L (JIRCAS, SOFRI); Vietnamese growers sprayed 7–9.5 L. Common labels: 10, 15, 20 or 25% WP, or SC.
