        Adjust stage lengths (days)

        Defaults: PBZ to flowering is 60–122 days, typically 78 (Kozai 2024; Wiangsamut 2022; Tri 2011, where spray to harvest was 191–212 days). Flowering to harvest is 120–127 days for 'Monthong' (Sangwanangkul 1998) about 97 for 'Ri6' (Tran et al. 2019) and about 90 for 'Musang King' (Duong et al. 2025). The ± bands on Ri6 and Musang King are planning buffers we added. Change these if you have your own orchard records or grow another cultivar.

        Paclobutrazol in durian: every trial side by side

        PBZ is the most widely used off-season tool, but the trial results are not uniform. Positive results came from foliar sprays at 1,000–1,600 ppm on fully expanded second-flush leaves, followed by dry weather.
